Wine, without the occasion.

Sēmeion is Greek for “sign.” In the Greek of the New Testament it is also the word for a miracle, and the first one told is water turned into wine at a wedding feast.

We love that idea: wine was never meant to wait for a special occasion. It was meant to show up where people gather, and make the moment feel a little bigger. Sēmeion takes that spirit and puts it in a beer bottle. It’s a light sparkling wine at about 5% ABV, easy to open without ceremony and easy to enjoy over an afternoon.

The small everyday miracle is the setting. Crack one open at a concert, a tailgate, a picnic, or a Tuesday dinner, and wine stops being an occasion and becomes part of the moment.

The label

A path winds toward a guiding star inside an arched window, set on a field of red vine pattern and finished in gold. It’s a nod to the old idea of following a sign to something worth finding.
